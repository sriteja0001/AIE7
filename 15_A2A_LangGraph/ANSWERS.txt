❓ Question #1:
What are the core components of an AgentCard?

A. The core components of an AgentCard are its basic info (name, description, url, version), input/output modes (e.g. what content types it accepts), and agent skills (essentially
the specific tools it has). 

❓ Question #2:
Why is A2A (and other such protocols) important in your own words?

A. A2A is important because it allows different AI agents talk to each other and work together, similar to how APIs let different apps "communicate." This means we can build specialized
AI agents who can then collaborate without involving humans in the loop, leading to more streamlined and efficient processes.
